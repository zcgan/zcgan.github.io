This archived page records a Fall 2022 offering at HKUST(GZ).

## Course focus

The course used case studies in materials science and engineering to develop mathematical models, analyze their assumptions, and interpret numerical results. Topics included collective motion and aggregation, heat conduction and elasticity, charge transport, plasmonic effects, and biochemical kinetics.

## Offering

Fall 2022, HKUST(GZ). Course materials were provided through Canvas during the offering.
