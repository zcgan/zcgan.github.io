## Leadership and service

I organize seminars, workshops, and minisymposia in scientific computing, electrostatics, materials science, and machine learning. Current service includes the Youth Editorial Board of the <em>Journal of Materials Informatics</em> and selected refereeing for journals in numerical analysis, computational physics, and materials science.

Selected institutional roles include UG coordinator for the Materials Science and Engineering major, membership of the Senate Committee on Teaching and Learning Quality, and curriculum development in the Advanced Materials Thrust.
