## Research programs

<p class="section-intro">The group develops numerical methods around specific physical questions. Two programs form the core of the work; data-driven PDE computation is a newer direction.</p>

<div class="research-programs">
  <section class="research-program research-program-core">
    <p class="program-status">Core program</p>
    <h3>Long-range interactions</h3>
    <p>We study how to compute electrostatic and hydrodynamic interactions when confinement, periodicity, and dielectric contrast make standard methods unreliable or expensive.</p>
    <p class="program-methods">Ewald methods, random batching, boundary integrals, error analysis</p>
    <p class="program-links"><a href="{{ '/科研论文成果汇总/published/2025_SISC_RBE2D_GanGaoLiangXu.pdf' | relative_url }}">Random-batch Ewald</a> <a href="{{ '/科研论文成果汇总/published/2025_JCompPhys_Q2D_GanGaoLiangXu.pdf' | relative_url }}">Quasi-2D Coulomb algorithm</a></p>
  </section>
  <section class="research-program research-program-core">
    <p class="program-status">Core program</p>
    <h3>Soft and active materials</h3>
    <p>We connect interaction models to attraction, packing, broken symmetry, and collective motion in polarizable and active particle systems.</p>
    <p class="program-methods">Electrostatic theory, particle simulation, reduced physical models</p>
    <p class="program-links"><a href="{{ '/科研论文成果汇总/published/2025_LCA_SoftMatter_DuanGanChan.pdf' | relative_url }}">Polarizable spheres</a> <a href="{{ '/科研论文成果汇总/published/2026_JCP_ConfinedDipoleColumns_DuanGanMohrbachChanMessina.pdf' | relative_url }}">Confined dipolar columns</a></p>
  </section>
  <section class="research-program research-program-emerging">
    <div>
      <p class="program-status">Emerging program</p>
      <h3>Data-driven PDEs</h3>
    </div>
    <div>
      <p>Current projects examine operator learning and reduced-order models for PDE dynamics with limited data. The public record currently consists mainly of accepted conference work, preprints, and manuscripts under review.</p>
